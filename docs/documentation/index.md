# What is NUVA ?
NUVA, the Unified Nomenclature of Vaccine, is a community tool for vaccine codification experts across the world to represent in a common, consistent and documented way the history of administered vaccines.

It is provided under the [Creative Commons CC-BY license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## What need does it serve ?
There are already many code systems for medicinal products. Yet, contrary to almost all products, vaccines have a lifetime effect. People need to keep track of the vaccines they have received through their lifetime, while the medicinal products codes are rarely valid for more than a few years and only within a given country or region.

The purpose of NUVA is to bridge the gap across existing codifications, both across countries and in the very long term.

## What does it consist of ?
THe NUVA environment consists of:
  - the [core NUVA ontology](core/core.md), that is a structured knowledge base formed by  concepts for administered vaccines, concepts for their valences, that is their functions in training the immunity system, and relationships among these concepts.
  - additional layers, that enrich the code NUVA with further information. The two essential layers types are:
    - [language layers](layers/language_layers.md), that transcribe the wording for the NUVA concepts from English to another language.
	- [alignment layers](layers/alignment_layers.md), that provide for each code in an existing representation of vaccines their equivalent NUVA code.
  - an [organisation](organisation/organisation.md) to reference, update and distribute the NUVA core and the additional layers.
  - [supporting tools](tools/tools.md) for users and for contributors
